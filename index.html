Why now

The knowledge is already built. It's just locked up.

Most legal organizations are sitting on years of automation work — and facing pressure to "do AI" — without a bridge between the two.

Aging template libraries

HotDocs and similar systems hold thousands of carefully built rules, often maintained by one person who is close to retiring.

AI without guardrails

Generative tools produce confident drafts that miss required clauses, jurisdictional rules, and the firm's own standards.

No one owns the bridge

IT doesn't know the law, lawyers don't know the logic, and vendors only know their product. That gap is our specialty.
